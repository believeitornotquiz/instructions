## Overview
This guide explains how to set up and run quizzes using the **Believe It or Not?** platform. We strongly recommend you use the **app** to run your quiz so you can preload content, and use the **website** for marking and controlling the presentation.

![Main menu showing synced quizzes](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/believeitornotquiz/instructions/refs/heads/main/figure%201.png)
*Figure 1: Main menu with synced/unsynced quizzes and resource links.*

<a id="setup"></a>
## Setup
1. **Do you have an account?** — If not, contact <admin@believeitornot.co.nz>.
2. **Download and install the app** (to be able to download the quiz and run it from your computer): <https://tinyurl.com/BION-builds>
3. **Website access**: <https://quiz.believeitornot.co.nz/>
4. Use the **website** to log in on any device for marking controls. You may display the quiz via the website if venue Wi‑Fi is reliable, but **only the app** can **preload all content to enable playing it offline**.
5. **Marking and the scoreboard always require an internet connection**, even when using the app.
6. Once a quiz is started, it becomes **live across all devices** — actions on any device are immediately reflected everywhere.
7. You can control slides on a phone while displaying the quiz on a laptop, and **have multiple people mark simultaneously**.

<a id="main-menu"></a>
## Main Menu
On login, the control panel lists quizzes available to your account.
* **Start Quiz** — begin a game.
* **Sync for Offline** (app only) — download quiz content to your computer.
* **Download Resources** — get the PDF Q&A booklet for that week's quiz. This may not be available for all quizzes.
* **Reset Session** - reset your account, closing all games. Use this when you encounter bugged games or greyed out buttons. Fixes most issues but will close any active games, losing their scores.

<a id="running-the-quiz"></a>
## Running the Quiz
![Quiz control panel showing Start and Sync options](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/believeitornotquiz/instructions/refs/heads/main/Select%20Quiz%20Mode.png)

*Figure 3: Three options when starting a game. If unsure, use Option 1.*

After pressing **Start Quiz**, you will see three options to launch your game.
* **Option 1:** — Teams join online + answer using the regular pen and paper booklets. The default option, teams will join the quiz via a QR code enabling participation in our leaderboards.
* **Option 2:** — Mobile phone gameplay. An under construction mode intended for special quiz events. Can be used for regular quiz nights but is not recommended. Teams answer questions on their phones and questions are automatically marked by the system.
* **Option 3:** — No online activity at all. Creates a fully manual quiz game without any internet connectivity beyond marking. Leaderboard participation is completely disabled.

Once the game is launched, you'll be greeted with three main tabs on your game screen.

<a id="teams-tab"></a>
### Teams Tab
1. Use the **Teams** tab to manage participating teams.
2. Press **Add Team** to add a new team manually. This will not enable participation on the Leaderboards for that team.
3. Edit any teams using the **pen** button; remove with the **rubbish bin**. You can do this at any time, even after the game starts, and even where they have joined online. Fix incorrect Jokers, alter team names, etc.
4. In the **Add Team** dialog, enter the team name and amount of players on the team, assign **Joker rounds**, then **double‑click Save** to finish adding them.

![Add Team dialog with Joker rounds](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/believeitornotquiz/instructions/refs/heads/main/figure%203.png)
*Figure 4: Add Team dialog; assign Joker rounds before saving.*

<a id="marking-tab"></a>
### Marking Tab
1. Select the **round** in the left dropdown; when a team hands in their sheet, select the **team** in the right dropdown to mark efficiently (especially on mobile).
2. Tap the **answer buttons** for each correctly answered question.
3. Tap **Answers** to view full answers for the round and any host info.
4. Tap **Preview Scoreboard** to check scores without showing them publicly. Note: ties will not be sorted on the preview, but will be on the main scoreboard.
5. Multiple people can mark concurrently on different devices; solo hosts often find phone marking fastest.
6. **The List** is marked differently, simply assign the team their points value for the round.
7. **Tie‑breaks:** In the tie‑break section, assign the **highest points to the nearest answer**, next highest to the second closest, and so on. The system will handle the rankings for you, i.e. third place teams will only be sorted against third place teams even if given a higher tiebreak score than a higher placing team.

<a id="presentation-tab"></a>
### Presentation Tab

1. Use **Presentation** to display and control the quiz.
2. Click **Launch Window** to open the quiz presentation in a new window; press **F11** to enter full‑screen and show this window on your display(s).
3. Use **Left/Right arrows** (keyboard or clicker) to move through slides. On multimedia slides, the first click **ends playback**; the second click **advances** the slide.
4. **Media controls** (mute/pause) are active when audio/video is present. If you advance with media muted or paused, later slides may retain that state—**unmute/unpause** to avoid missing audio.
5. Use **Jump to round** to navigate.
6. Tap **Display Scoreboard** at any time; an animation will scroll through team scores.
7. Use **Back Soon** during short breaks or final marking—prefer a slide with music for ambience.
8. Use **Prize Wheel** to give away random spot prizes with some dramatic flair. There are two modes: a team mode which will automatically add the teams to the wheel, and a participant mode that will allow an individual from the winning team to win.
9. **Finalise Marking** immediately shows the **trophy animation scoreboard** (can be undone).
10. **End Quiz** permanently ends the game and **will erase current marking**—use with care.

<a id="offline-mode"></a>
## Offline Mode
- **Sync for Offline** is only available in the **app** and preloads quiz content to your computer.
- **Marking and the scoreboard require an internet connection** even when content is preloaded. Switch to manual scoring if connectivity is lost.

<a id="tips-best-practices"></a>
## Tips & Best Practices
- Use your **phone** for fast marking while presenting from a laptop.
- Before advancing slides with media, confirm **mute/pause** states to avoid silent playback later.
- Test venue **Wi‑Fi**; if spotty, rely on the **app** with offline sync and plan for manual scoring.

<a id="faq"></a>
## FAQ
<details>
  <summary>Can I run a quiz completely offline?</summary>
  You can preload content using the app, but marking and the scoreboard still require internet.
</details>
<details>
  <summary>What happens if I press End Quiz?</summary>
  The current game ends permanently and you will lose your marking. Consider using Back Soon or Finalise Marking first.
</details>
<details>
  <summary>How do tie-breaks work?</summary>
  Assign points based on closest answers; the system ranks teams automatically.
</details>
